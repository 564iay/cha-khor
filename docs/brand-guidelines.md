# The Cha Khor - Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity
**Name:** The Cha Khor
**Tagline:** Tehatta's premier dining destination.
**Concept:** A premium family restaurant offering authentic Indian flavors and vibrant Indo-Chinese dishes in an unforgettable family atmosphere.

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone
- **Voice:** Professional, welcoming, and premium.
- **Tone:** Cinematic, warm, and inviting. We speak with a sense of pride in our culinary heritage and community roots.
- **Messaging Framework:** 
  - Primary: "Experience Tehatta's premier dining destination."
  - Secondary: "Authentic Indian flavors, vibrant Indo-Chinese dishes, and an unforgettable family atmosphere."

## 3. Visual Identity

### Color Palette (Cinematic Theme)

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Usage |
|------|------------|----------|-------|
| Primary Background | Charcoal Black | `#1C1C1C` | Main site background, setting a cinematic feel |
| Secondary Background | Deep Burgundy | `#4A1525` | Section breaks, accents, footer |
| Primary Text | Warm Ivory | `#F5F1E8` | Main typography, high contrast against dark bg |
| Secondary Text | Muted Ivory | `#C8C0B8` | Subtitles, supporting text |
| Accent | Champagne Gold | `#D4AF37` | Buttons, highlights, important borders |
| Muted | Warm Gray | `#918A84` | Disabled states, subtle borders |
| Border | Dark Gray | `#2A2A2A` | Dividers, subtle structuring |

### Typography

- **Display Typeface:** Cormorant Garamond (`--font-cormorant`)
  - Usage: All headings (`h1` - `h6`), Hero text, and large stylized text.
  - Vibe: Elegant, premium, classic.
- **Body Typeface:** Karla (`--font-karla`)
  - Usage: Body copy, paragraphs, buttons, navigation links.
  - Vibe: Clean, modern, legible.

## 4. Logo Usage
- **Primary Logo:** Always ensure sufficient clear space. Use the Champagne Gold accent or Warm Ivory for single-color applications on dark backgrounds.
- **Do NOT:** Distort the logo, change its aspect ratio, or use unapproved colors.

## 5. UI Elements
- **Buttons:** 
  - Primary: Champagne Gold background, Charcoal Black text (`bg-accent text-background`).
  - Outline: Transparent with Champagne Gold border, matching hover states (`border-accent text-accent`).
- **Transitions:** Use smooth, slow transitions (`duration-500` or `duration-700`) to match the premium, cinematic feel.
