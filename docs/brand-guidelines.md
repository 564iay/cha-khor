# The Cha Khor - Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity
**Name:** The Cha Khor
**Tagline:** Tehatta's premier dining destination.
**Concept:** A premium family restaurant offering authentic Indian flavors and vibrant Indo-Chinese dishes in an unforgettable family atmosphere.

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone
- **Voice:** Professional, welcoming, and grounded.
- **Tone:** Peaceful, earthy, and highly welcoming. We speak with a sense of pride in our culinary heritage and community roots, ensuring families feel right at home.
- **Messaging Framework:** 
  - Primary: "Experience Tehatta's premier dining destination."
  - Secondary: "Authentic Indian flavors, vibrant Indo-Chinese dishes, and an unforgettable family atmosphere."

## 3. Visual Identity

### Color Palette (Earth & Clay Theme)

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Usage |
|------|------------|----------|-------|
| Primary Background | Warm Pearl | `#FDFBF7` | Main site background, light, airy, and peaceful |
| Secondary Background | Warm Sand | `#F4EFE6` | Section breaks, soft contrast |
| Primary Text | Deep Espresso | `#382F2D` | Main typography, high contrast, softer than pure black |
| Secondary Text | Muted Cocoa | `#6B5E5B` | Subtitles, supporting text |
| Accent | Soft Terracotta | `#C47357` | Buttons, highlights, important borders, earthy warmth |
| Muted | Sandstone | `#D8CFC4` | Disabled states, subtle borders |
| Border | Soft Border | `#E6DED5` | Dividers, subtle structuring |

### Typography

- **Display Typeface:** Cormorant Garamond (`--font-cormorant`)
  - Usage: All headings (`h1` - `h6`), Hero text, and large stylized text.
  - Vibe: Elegant, premium, classic.
- **Body Typeface:** Karla (`--font-karla`)
  - Usage: Body copy, paragraphs, buttons, navigation links.
  - Vibe: Clean, modern, highly legible.

## 4. Logo Usage
- **Primary Logo:** Always ensure sufficient clear space. Use the Soft Terracotta accent or Deep Espresso for single-color applications on light backgrounds.
- **Do NOT:** Distort the logo, change its aspect ratio, or use unapproved colors.

## 5. UI Elements
- **Buttons:** 
  - Primary: Soft Terracotta background, Warm Pearl text (`bg-accent text-background`).
  - Outline: Transparent with Soft Terracotta border, matching hover states (`border-accent text-accent`).
- **Transitions:** Use smooth, slow transitions (`duration-500` or `duration-700`) to match the premium, peaceful feel.
